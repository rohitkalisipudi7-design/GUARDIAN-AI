# GUARDIAN-AI

Guardian AI is a personal digital-safety companion designed to help individuals — particularly students and young people navigating online spaces — recognize, document, and respond to cyberbullying, harassment, sextortion, scams, and other forms of digital abuse. The product combines a conversational AI companion with a suite of detection, evidence-collection, and response tools, aiming to give at-risk users both emotional support and practical, actionable protection.

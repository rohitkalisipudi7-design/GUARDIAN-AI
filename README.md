# GUARDIAN AI - Personal Digital Safety Companion

Guardian AI is a personal digital-safety companion designed to help individuals — particularly students and young people navigating online spaces — recognize, document, and respond to cyberbullying, harassment, sextortion, scams, and other forms of digital abuse. The product combines a conversational AI companion with a suite of detection, evidence-collection, and response tools, aiming to give at-risk users both emotional support and practical, actionable protection.

## Core Features (MVP)
* **AI Companion**: An always-available, judgment-free AI that provides personalized safety plans and digital-safety education.
* **Threat Detection**: Analyze pasted text, chat exports, or screenshots to detect risks like cyberbullying, manipulation, sextortion, and scams.
* **Evidence Vault**: An encrypted local vault to safely store screenshots, chat exports, and other evidence, complete with timelines and secure export options.
* **Emergency Center**: One-tap trusted-contact notifications, safety checklists, and a helpline directory.
* **Learning Hub**: Educational content on topics like privacy, identity theft, safe dating, and financial scams.

## How to Install

Guardian AI is an Android application. Follow these steps to build and install it on your device:

1. **Clone the repository**:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/rohitkalisipudi7-design/GUARDIAN-AI.git
   ```
2. **Open in Android Studio**:
   Launch Android Studio and select `File > Open`, then navigate to the cloned `GUARDIAN-AI` directory.
3. **Sync Project with Gradle Files**:
   Wait for Android Studio to index the files and sync Gradle. If prompted, download any missing SDK components.
4. **Run the App**:
   Connect your physical Android device (with USB Debugging enabled) or start an Android Emulator. Click the **Run** button (green play icon) in Android Studio to build and install the app.

## How to Use

1. **Onboarding & Setup**: When you first launch Guardian AI, you can set up your emergency trusted contacts and personalize your AI companion's settings.
2. **Chat with the AI Companion**: Use the main chat interface to ask questions about online privacy, ask for advice on handling digital abuse, or receive emotional support.
3. **Analyze Threats**: If you receive suspicious messages or emails, paste the text or upload a screenshot. Guardian AI will assess the risk and suggest immediate next steps.
4. **Save Evidence**: In high-risk situations, use the Encrypted Evidence Vault to store screenshots and text logs securely. These can be exported later if you decide to report the incident to authorities or platform administrators.
5. **Get Emergency Help**: In urgent situations, use the Emergency Center to quickly alert your trusted contacts with your safety status.
